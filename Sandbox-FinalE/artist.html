Artist Info

Lady Gaga

Pop / Dance-pop

Lady Gaga grew up in New York City and played piano from a young age. She broke through with her first album, The Fame, and hits like "Just Dance" and "Poker Face". She is known for bold fashion, big live shows and a voice that moves between dance-pop, jazz and ballads. She has won many Grammy Awards and an Oscar for "Shallow".

  • Real name: Stefani Joanne Angelina Germanotta
  • From: New York City, USA
  • Born: March 28, 1986
  • Song on this site: "Applause" from ARTPOP (2013)
  • Official site: ladygaga.com

George Michael

Pop / Soul

George Michael first became famous as one half of Wham! before starting a solo career. His album Faith won the Grammy for Album of the Year, and he became one of the best-selling artists in the world. He was known for his smooth, soulful voice and for writing and producing much of his own music.

  • Real name: Georgios Kyriacos Panayiotou
  • From: London, England
  • Born: June 25, 1963 (died December 25, 2016)
  • Song on this site: "Cowboys And Angels" on Ladies & Gentlemen: The Best of George Michael (1998)
  • Official site: georgemichael.com

Wham!

Pop / 80s Pop

Wham! was an English duo made up of school friends George Michael and Andrew Ridgeley. They had big 80s hits like "Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go" and "Last Christmas". In 1985 they became one of the first Western pop groups to perform in China. The duo split in 1986, but "Last Christmas" still comes back on the charts every December.

  • Members: George Michael, Andrew Ridgeley
  • From: Bushey, Hertfordshire, England
  • Active: 1981 – 1986
  • Song on this site: "Last Christmas" (1984)

Maroon 5

Pop Rock

Maroon 5 is a band from Los Angeles led by singer Adam Levine. They started out as Kara's Flowers and became famous with their first album as Maroon 5, Songs About Jane, and the hits "This Love" and "She Will Be Loved". Their album Overexposed gave them "Payphone" and "One More Night", which stayed at number one in the US for weeks.

  • Lead singer: Adam Levine
  • From: Los Angeles, California, USA
  • Formed: 1994 (as Kara's Flowers), Maroon 5 since 2001
  • Song on this site: "One More Night" from Overexposed (2012)
  • Official site: maroon5.com

Edward Maya & Vika Jigulina

Dance / House

Edward Maya is a Romanian DJ, producer and songwriter. He made "Stereo Love" with singer Vika Jigulina, and its catchy accordion melody turned it into a dance hit across Europe and the United States. The song is still one of the best-known dance tracks of its time.

  • Real name: Eduard Marian Ilie (Edward Maya)
  • From: Bucharest, Romania
  • Born: June 29, 1986
  • Song on this site: "Stereo Love" (2009)

Michael Jackson

Pop / R&B

Known as the "King of Pop", Michael Jackson started singing as a child with his brothers in the Jackson 5. As a solo artist he made Thriller, the best-selling album of all time, and changed music videos and dance forever with moves like the moonwalk. He won 13 Grammy Awards during his career.

  • Real name: Michael Joseph Jackson
  • From: Gary, Indiana, USA
  • Born: August 29, 1958 (died June 25, 2009)
  • Song on this site: "The Lady in My Life" from Thriller (1982)
  • Official site: michaeljackson.com
